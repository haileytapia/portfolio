## About me

I started out in technical writing obsessed with perfecting individual sentences. Today, I lead documentation strategy, shaping not only what gets written but also how it's organized, connected, navigated, and understood.

I still care deeply about individual articles, but I now consider how they fit together to serve dual audiences: people who read and use the content directly and AI agents that find, understand, and use it on their behalf.

My professional experience spans both hardware and software, including ownership of a developer-focused content portfolio at Microsoft. For my full background, see my [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/haileytapia/).
